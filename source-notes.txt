SOURCE NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

Original 28 categories: supplied original HTML and first names.js, verified entry-by-entry.

New larger modern pools: Faker 40.1.2 localized person-provider name arrays; locale(s) named in the JavaScript comments. No runtime dependency on Faker. These are illustrative name pools, not census data. Some locales contain shared cultural names, historical names, personal names used in second-name slots, and gender-dependent family names. Romanization of non-Latin scripts uses ICU Any-Latin and may differ from customary spellings.
Faker: https://github.com/joke2k/faker
Documentation: https://faker.readthedocs.io/en/stable/

Other additions: manually curated examples from general knowledge; not each independently verified. The lists vary in size. Geographic usage notes are approximate, not statistics. Some Indigenous groups are represented by public civil-name forms rather than traditional or ceremonial names. Historical categories are explicitly revival pools, and their second-name slots are adapted for fictional future use. Smaller traditions for which confident three-field examples were not available have not been included.

Background on geographic usage: https://www.behindthename.com/support/usages.php
Background on ancient names: https://www.behindthename.com/names/usage/ancient
Background on Inuit naming and imposed surnames: https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/indigenous-history/indigenous-terminology.html
Background on naming systems: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12819479/
